Act III / About

Between logic
and imagination.

I’m Andrew Zhang, a researcher and creator trained across theatre, computer science, and mathematics at UC Santa Barbara.

My creative practice includes directing, acting, and Shakespearean performance. These experiences shape how I think about bodies, language, collaboration, and the subtle ways a space can direct attention.

My research and technical work explores how computational systems can create and study embodied, interactive, and narrative experiences. I’m currently developing Tempest VR, an immersive adaptation and research project centered on Prospero’s first encounter with Ariel.
