The Tempest at UCSB Lagoon

The Tempest by William Shakespeare
Naked Shakes, UCSB
Commencement Green, UCSB & Elings Park, Santa Barbara

Trinculo · Directed by Irwin Appel

In summer 2025, I played Trinculo in The Tempest in front of the beautiful lagoon at UCSB. Being a clown character has taught me focus, precision, and commitment. Huge thanks to my scene partners -- Vivian Oxley (Stephano), Claire Rubers (1/2 of Caliban), and Veda Arndt-Schreiber (other 1/2 of Caliban). Together we made the isle full of noises and laughter!
