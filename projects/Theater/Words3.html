Words, Words, Words

By David Ives
UCSB Senior Directing Program
Studio Theater, UCSB

Directed by Andrew Zhang

Cast

  • McKenna Hill · Kafka
  • Sadie Joy · Swift
  • Alexander Beay · Milton

I presented Words, Words, Words as my first full one-act play produced by UCSB. In this play, three monkeys -- named Kafka, Swift, and Milton -- sit in front of their typewriters and try to write Hamlet. What is Hamlet, though? They ask. Scratch scratch. They don't know, but they continue to type.

As a director, I enjoy working on stories that are bold and challenge our imagination.
